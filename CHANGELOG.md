# Changelog

## v1.3.0 (2026-11-24)
- **Built-In Asset Library (`1,220` Assets)**: 1,040 stickers & badges (10 categories × 8 styles), 120 synthesized 48kHz stereo WAV sound effects & music loops, and 60 cinema color filters — 1-click direct insert inside NovaCut Studio.
- **Full Light & Dark Themes**: Complete dynamic theme palette across all panels, timeline tracks, hint bar, toasts, and modals.
- **Arabic RTL & English Bilingual UI**: Native DirectWrite RTL alignment, zero slider/card overlap, and localized controls.

## v1.2.0
- **Hybrid 13-Model Local AI Bridge (`CapCutVS_AI/ai_worker.py`)**: Optional offline sidecar support for LM Studio (`127.0.0.1:1234`), Whisper (`faster-whisper` / `whisper.cpp`), BiRefNet/`rembg`, SAM 2, OpenCV (RANSAC stabilization, homography, 6-DOF), Real-ESRGAN, RIFE, LaMa Inpainting, Demucs, GFPGAN, CodeFormer, Depth Anything V2, MediaPipe, and Wav2Lip.
- **Zero-Freeze Async AI Worker**: All AI and CV operations execute on background threads.

## v1.0.0
- **Core Native C++20 NLE Engine**: Direct2D + DirectWrite + WIC + Windows Media Foundation GPU H.264/HEVC/AV1/GIF/PNG Seq/Alpha PNG exporter.
- **Cinema NLE, Pro Color Scopes & Audio DAW**: J/L-Cut, Slip/Slide, Compound & Nested sequences, MultiCam, Custom Speed Ramp, Disk Proxy Generator, Live Histogram/Waveform/Vectorscope/False Color, 8-Band HSL, RGB Curves, `.cube` LUT Import/Export, 10 Photo Retouch Brushes, and 100% Preview/Export Audio DSP parity.
